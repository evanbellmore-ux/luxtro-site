A quiet game of light

Luxtro

Place mirrors, splitters, prisms and combiners to steer colored light into every goal. This page is help and contact for the game on iPhone, iPad and Android.

Contact

For questions, problems or ideas, email nbtguild@gmail.com. It helps to say which device you play on and, for a puzzle, its chapter and number.

Common questions

What is free, and what do the packs add?

The 14 tutorials, the 90-puzzle Main game and Speed run are free, and so is the first puzzle of every pack. The Portals, Machinery, Linked and Crystals packs each add 50 puzzles built around a new mechanism, for a one-time purchase. All four can also be bought together.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the main menu, tap More levels, then tap Restore purchases. Packs bought with the same Apple Account open again on any iPhone or iPad, and packs bought with the same Google account open again on any Android device. On Android they also come back on their own when Luxtro starts.

Is my progress backed up?

Your progress and settings are saved on your device, not online. If you delete Luxtro they are deleted with it, but bought packs come back through Restore purchases.

How do I ask for a refund?

Apple or Google handles every payment. On iPhone and iPad, request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, follow Google Play's refund steps.

Privacy

Luxtro has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
