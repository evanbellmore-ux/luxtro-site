Privacy policy

Effective November 12, 2026

Luxtro is a puzzle game for iPhone, iPad and Android phones and tablets. It is made by Evan Bellmore and published on Google Play by NBT Ventures LLC ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play it.

What the game sends

Luxtro has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The game does not send information about you, your device or how you play to us, and we have no servers that could receive it.

On Android, purchases go through Google Play's billing system. To use it, Luxtro includes Google's Play Billing Library, which may send Google diagnostic information about the billing service, such as whether a request to Google Play succeeded. Google uses it to run Google Play, and we do not receive it.

If you turn on Share With App Developers on an iPhone or iPad, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage figures. Google Play may likewise give us anonymous crash reports and install statistics. These come from Apple and Google, not from the game, and do not identify you.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress, best scores, settings and the packs you own on your device, so that you can carry on where you left off. Luxtro never sends this information anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it, and we hold no other information about you that could be deleted.

Purchases

Puzzle packs are bought through Apple's App Store on iPhone and iPad, and through Google Play on Android. Apple or Google processes the payment, and we never see your payment details, your Apple Account or your Google Account. The game only learns from the store which packs your account owns. Their handling of your information is covered by Apple's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

Children

Luxtro collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to nbtguild@gmail.com.
