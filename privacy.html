Privacy policy

Effective November 9, 2026

Luxtro is a puzzle game for iPhone and iPad made by Evan Bellmore ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play it.

We collect no data

Luxtro has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The game does not send information about you, your device or how you play to us or to anyone else.

If you turn on Share With App Developers in your device's settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage figures. These come from Apple, not from the game, and do not identify you.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress, best scores, settings and the packs you own on your device, so that you can carry on where you left off. Luxtro never sends this information anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

Puzzle packs are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment, and we never see your payment details or your Apple Account. The game only learns from Apple which packs your account owns. Apple's handling of your information is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Luxtro collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to nbtguild@gmail.com.
